## Article 1 Corrections and Sources

Medicare health coverage is not a funeral-expense benefit. Do not conflate medical advance-care planning with funeral arrangements, or claim that all support stops at death: CMS hospice bereavement support can continue afterward. The SSA $255 lump-sum death payment is separate, limited to eligible survivors, generally requires application within two years, and is not automatically payable to whoever covers the funeral. Monthly survivor benefits are a separate program. VA benefits have their own eligibility; do not claim that no government assistance exists.

## Foundation To Preserve

- Seventeen content routes: home, quote, about, ten cities, and four guides. City and guide templates generate known paths and use `notFound()` for unknown slugs.
- Distinct title/description data and canonicals; one H1 per page template; semantic headings, navigation, disclosure footer, and internal links from home to all ten cities and four guides.
- System fonts, an 18px body baseline, readable spacing, keyboard-oriented controls, and mostly server-rendered components. The quote form is client-side.
- WebSite and Article JSON-LD describe the site/content without fabricated InsuranceAgency status, reviews, address, or carrier relationships.
- The hosted demo uses fixed sample contact details and browser-only interaction. The quote API returns 403 before reading a payload unless the explicitly local synthetic-test gate is enabled; Vercel cannot enable that local exception. A demo success is not a saved lead.

## 2. Evidence And Local Value

City prose differs, but landmarks alone do not demonstrate service expertise. Funeral-price assertions lack visible source dates and methodology. County/ZIP fields simplify areas that can span multiple counties or postal boundaries; verify each before presenting it as coverage geography. No full landmark or boundary verification is claimed by this audit.

`lib/cities.ts`, `lib/guides.ts`, and their templates have no dedicated reference, author-profile, or editorial-review-date fields. Add evidence to the content model and visible pages only when real sources and reviewers exist. The pending founder panel in `app/about/page.tsx:69-86` is truthful but unfinished; a later consumer-facing revision should emphasize a verified mission and process without inventing a biography or credentials. Existing About cost claims also need review.

## 3. Metadata And Social Presentation

`app/layout.tsx:7-26` supplies a title template. Applying it to source title data produces Winder 94 characters, Auburn 90, and Hoschton 85. These are calculated lengths, not measured search-result truncation or a ranking penalty. Tighten titles around each page's purpose rather than a rigid character rule.

Buford's title includes quotes although the current demo cannot deliver an actual quote. No Open Graph image or Twitter card configuration is present. About and quote define canonicals but no page-specific Open Graph URL, so the root URL may be inherited; verify rendered metadata before changing it. Keep titles and descriptions truthful to the service available now.

## 4. Launch Controls And Sitemap

`lib/site.ts` intentionally sets prelaunch mode and disables public intake. `app/layout.tsx` emits noindex directives. `app/robots.ts` disallows crawling unconditionally, so changing prelaunch mode alone will not enable normal crawling. These controls are intentional for this demo; do not remove them in an SEO cleanup. Robots controls are not authentication or a privacy boundary, and blocking crawling can prevent a crawler from reading a page's noindex directive.

The proposed `peachstatefinalexpense.com` remains the canonical and sitemap target, despite ownership being unconfirmed. Verify ownership and the intended production origin before any actual launch. `app/sitemap.ts` gives every page the generation-time date as `lastModified`; use genuine significant-update dates or omit that field. [Google's sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap) calls for accurate modification dates and ignores priority/change-frequency values. No indexing setup or submission is authorized here.

## 5. Internal Links And Navigation

`app/cities/[slug]/page.tsx:57` selects the first six other entries in array order, not verified nearby communities. Auburn, Braselton, and Hoschton never appear as targets in this module, although homepage links mean they are not orphaned. Replace the selection later with a deliberate neighbor map and useful contextual guide links.

The guide breadcrumb in `app/guides/[slug]/page.tsx` labels a link to FAQ as Guides. Use truthful breadcrumb wording or create a real guide hub when justified. Consider on-page navigation for long guides. Avoid thin city/age multiplication; [Google's doorway-abuse policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#doorway-abuse) is relevant to pages built mainly to funnel similar queries to the same destination. This is a preventive recommendation, not a finding of a Google penalty.

## 6. Structured Data

Preserve truthful WebSite/Article markup. BreadcrumbList, accurate editorial dates, appropriate article images, and a real author/reviewer profile can improve descriptive completeness when those facts exist. [Google's Article documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article) is the implementation reference. Do not fabricate InsuranceAgency/LocalBusiness data, ratings, addresses, credentials, or partnerships.

Keep useful FAQs for readers. [Google's May 2026 update](https://developers.google.com/search/updates#may-2026) says FAQ rich results stopped appearing May 7, 2026. Do not propose FAQ markup as a promised rich-result or ranking win.

## 7. Before Actual Intake

Clarify that Matt is the recipient if that is the approved operation, then specify the real contact method/window and quote process. `components/QuoteForm.tsx` is a fixed-sample demo, not that pipeline. Email is optional, and the local synthetic API in `app/api/quote/route.ts` does not require email when Email is the preferred channel; future live validation should require the chosen channel's contact details.

`components/Footer.tsx` links its privacy notice to the demo form. A real privacy page must reflect actual recipients, retention, deletion, and sharing practices. Consent must match the real workflow and receive appropriate review; it is not certified compliant here. Local JSONL storage and future alert integration points are not a durable production pipeline. Verify storage, delivery to the approved recipient, duplicates, errors, and privacy-preserving measurement before enabling anything.

Business Profile eligibility is a separate decision: [Google excludes lead-generation and online-only businesses](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036). A future licensed practice still needs to meet the actual eligibility criteria; a license or service-area label alone does not establish eligibility. No profile or account setup is part of this milestone.

## 8. Performance: What Was And Was Not Measured

Source-level positives include system fonts, mostly server components, and a reserved hero layout using `next/image`, `fill`, `sizes="100vw"`, and preload. The hero WebP is 138,614 bytes in the repository; this is not a measured network transfer size. No Lighthouse, CrUX, Core Web Vitals, ranking, traffic, or competition score was measured in this audit. Run a reproducible exact-build mobile performance check later, not an assumed speed claim.

## Recommended Sequence

1. Correct unsupported claims and fictional experience across existing pages; have the resulting content reviewed by the appropriate qualified person.
2. Ask Muse for a query-to-existing-page map with intent, research dates, sources, and uncertainty.
3. Make a focused metadata, references, cross-link, breadcrumb, and social-preview pass based on that map.
4. Independently retest mobile behavior, exact-build performance, and the demo form. Preserve noindex and intake-off controls.
5. Separately authorize and verify the real domain, business/licensing facts, consent/privacy, production storage, delivery, and eventual indexing/intake launch.
6. Expand only from verified demand and, after authorized setup, actual Search Console and qualified-lead results. No access or measurement setup is implied by this recommendation.
